CHAIN REACTION

A strategic multiplayer explosion game

🔵 Blue starts first

Quick Rules

  • Turn-Based: A tactical battle between two or more players.
  • Timers: Includes a total Game Timer and a rapid Turn Timer to keep the pressure high.
  • Scoring System: Points are calculated based on the number of pieces captured in a reaction and the number of chain reactions.
  • Win Condition: Game ends when the board has only elements of one player (or) the timer runs out.
